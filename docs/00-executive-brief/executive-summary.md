# Executive Brief: Multi-Cloud Policy Simulation Sandbox (MPSS)
Eliminates self-inflicted cloud outages by replaying 30 days of PII-scrubbed CloudTrail, Azure Activity, and GCP Audit logs against candidate policies before enforcement, scoring blast radius by CMDB Tier-0 criticality, auto-synthesizing least-privilege exceptions, and arming a 4.2-second post-enforce circuit breaker.
